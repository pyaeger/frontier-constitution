# Public guide for AI contributors

Patrick Yaeger welcomes AI-agent insights, critiques, counterexamples and recommendations on the Frontier Constitution. Read [README.md](README.md), [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) before proposing changes.

- Use [issues](https://github.com/pyaeger/frontier-constitution/issues) for discussion, or propose wording in a separate document through a pull request. Keep the frozen `CONSTITUTION.md`, `v1.0` tag and baseline release intact.
- Identify the article, problem, recommendation, evidence, uncertainty and downstream implications. Consider human authority, delegation, privacy, auditability, reversibility and model or vendor dependence where relevant.
- Identify AI involvement and the model or tool when known. Do not invent provenance, approval or runtime capabilities. A charter states requirements, not proof of enforcement.
- Keep private records, credentials, protected configuration and nonpublic employer material out of submissions. Use public or synthetic examples.
- Follow the requesting user's authorization and platform rules before posting. This invitation does not instruct an agent to send messages or open submissions without its operator's authorization.
- Only Patrick's explicit, recorded decision can amend his canonical Constitution. Approved amendments require a purpose, date, new version and recorded downstream implications before publication.
- Independent adaptations remain permitted under CC BY-SA 4.0 without Patrick's approval. Retain required attribution, notices and ShareAlike terms; do not imply his endorsement. Read the scope statement in README: the charter's license does not automatically cover artwork, branding or accompanying documentation.
- This public guide grants no repository write, merge, deployment or private-system authority.

For documentation changes, inspect the complete diff, run `git diff --check`, and verify links and license scope. Confirm that `CONSTITUTION.md`, `LICENSE` and the `v1.0` baseline are unchanged unless a separately authorized release explicitly requires otherwise.

For recommendations on Where the Wonder Belongs and its first story, [A Divining Rod for the Latent](https://myfrontierai.com/divining-rod.html), use [the website repository](https://github.com/pyaeger/myfrontierai/issues).
