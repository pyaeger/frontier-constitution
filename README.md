# The Frontier Constitution

A reusable personal-AI governance charter by **Patrick Yaeger**: human authority, evidence, explicit delegation, privacy, local capability, auditability and deliberate change.

**Version 1.0 · Public Draft · frozen public baseline**

[Read the Constitution](CONSTITUTION.md) · [Read on MyFrontierAI](https://myfrontierai.com/constitution.html) · [v1.0 release](https://github.com/pyaeger/frontier-constitution/releases/tag/v1.0) · [Suggest an improvement](CONTRIBUTING.md)

The charter describes how Patrick intends his independent Frontier project to be governed. It can also be adapted for another person's AI environment. A written charter states requirements; it does not prove that a system is operational or that its controls enforce them. Frontier's [dated capability evidence](https://myfrontierai.com/#capability-status) is assessed separately.

## Fork and adapt

1. Use GitHub's **Fork** action to create your own copy, or download the [v1.0 baseline](https://github.com/pyaeger/frontier-constitution/tree/v1.0).
2. Read the charter and the [license](LICENSE). Adapt the constitutional text to your own purpose, authority structure and environment.
3. Identify your adaptation and its owner clearly, record your changes and dates, retain attribution and license notices, and apply CC BY-SA 4.0 or another license permitted by its ShareAlike terms when sharing adapted material.
4. Evaluate implementation separately: define what must be enforced, who may act, what evidence demonstrates it and what happens when a control fails. The charter alone provides no implementation guarantee.

**Your independently licensed adaptation does not need Patrick's approval.** His approval requirement governs his canonical Constitution. Do not imply that he endorses your adaptation or extend its licensing to branding or other excluded material.
